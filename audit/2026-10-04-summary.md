## 2. Top actions

1. **Split `ArgParser::parseFlags_` and `Cli::getInput_`** (~170 lines each). Gain: readability of the two most complex parsers. Effort: medium, covered by the tests. *(quality 3.1)*
2. **Fix upstream the 14 `c2dmp-hsm` conversion warnings** and add an SPDX line in the sync `sed` step of `c2dmp-hsm` / `s.o.s`. Effort: small. *(quality 3.4, dependencies 4)*
3. **Run the `SharedMemory` client in a `fork`ed child in `ReadableLastOnly`**. Gain: a clean TSan run. Effort: small. *(bugs 3)*
4. **Decide on the unreachable `autoCast` cases** (`Settings.hpp:126-157`). Effort: small. *(tests 5)*
5. **Add error-code cases** to raise the branch coverage (74 %), starting with `Cli.cpp` and `Settings.cpp`. Effort: medium. *(tests 5)*
6. **Clean the root** (`sandbox/` for the scratch programs, delete `a.out` / `latex/`). Effort: minutes. *(quality 3.2)*
